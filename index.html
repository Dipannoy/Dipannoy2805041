About

I am a PhD candidate at the University of South Carolina building AI systems for materials discovery. Instead of years of costly lab trial-and-error, my models predict how a material will perform before it is ever made, and design new materials backward from the properties a product actually needs. I am now focused on generating materials on demand for targeted applications such as LEDs, semiconductors, and batteries.

Projects

Featured research

The core of my graduate research — University of South Carolina, 2022–present
01 Multi-Property Inverse Design

MEX-Multi

An energy-based model that predicts stability, band gap and stiffness from a single shared EquiformerV2 backbone instead of training one network per property. The properties are coupled only through that backbone, so training and inference see exactly the same input and the model maps the Pareto surface a designer actually has to choose from.

  • 68% lower prediction error
  • 3 properties, one backbone
Architecture & details First author · manuscript under review
02 Crystal Structure Prediction

MFCSP — Few-Step Generative CSP

A MeanFlow generator that learns the average velocity of the probability-flow trajectory rather than the instantaneous one, so a complete structure comes out in as little as one network evaluation where diffusion samplers integrate over thousands. The space group is used only as an auxiliary training signal, so generation stays formula-only.

  • 83.6% match rate vs. 78.3% prior best
  • 100× fewer network evaluations
Architecture & details Co-author · preprint, arXiv:2609.09799
03 Cooperative GNN for Materials

Adaptive Message-Passing GNN

A learnable scoring network assigns every atomic bond a continuous survival probability, and the regressor uses those as multiplicative edge gates rather than deleting edges outright — which keeps the whole thing differentiable. Bonds below a threshold are pruned, so the graph sparsifies with depth instead of over-smoothing.

  • 38% of bonds pruned
  • +2.8% band-gap accuracy
Architecture & details First author · manuscript under review
04 Conditional Generative Models

Property-Conditioned Crystal Generation

Adapter-based fine-tuning belongs to MatterGen and node-level conditioning belongs to CrystalGRW, so comparing the two as they ship would confound the conditioning scheme with the backbone underneath it. Porting each mechanism into the other framework — and holding the decoder GNN common — makes it an apples-to-apples comparison.

  • 5× better generation control
  • 3 conditioning routes on both frameworks
Architecture & details Contributor · work in progress
05 Domain Adaptation for Materials

Transfer Learning for Halide Perovskites

Weights learned on 500 simple perovskites are transferred into a data-poor double-perovskite space across SchNet, PAiNN and ALIGNN, then sharpened by uncertainty-driven active learning and used to screen a quarter of a million candidates down to a shortlist.

  • 222,960 candidates screened to 48
  • 1.6× lower error than training cold
Architecture & details First author · published, arXiv:2401.10998
